§1 · Summary

A typosquat is impersonating Claude for Windows.

On 2026-05-18, Elmahrosa's threat-monitoring identified a domain (clude-promo-offer.com) hosting a download page that visually impersonates the legitimate Claude desktop installer landing page. The domain name omits the letter a from claude — a classic typosquat designed to capture mistyped URLs and search-engine traffic for users looking to install Claude on Windows.

§4 · Indicators of Compromise." data-ar="تعرض الصفحة ملفًا تنفيذيًا لويندوز يُقدَّم على أنه مثبِّت كلود الرسمي. التحليل الأولي يشير إلى أن الملف غير موقَّع من Anthropic ويُظهر سلوكًا متسقًا مع حمولة سرقة معلومات: استخراج اعتمادات المتصفح، التثبت عبر مهام مجدولة، وحركة صادرة نحو نقطة قيادة وتحكم.">The page offers a Windows executable presented as the official Claude installer. Initial analysis indicates the executable is not signed by Anthropic and exhibits behavior consistent with an information-stealer payload: browser credential extraction, scheduled-task persistence, and outbound traffic to a command-and-control endpoint. Detailed indicators are under coordinated review with Anthropic and a third-party CERT — see §4 · Indicators of Compromise.

Elmahrosa is publishing this advisory in its capacity as a Member of the Claude Partner Network and as a courtesy to the broader Claude user community, particularly Arabic-speaking and MENA users who are common targets of typosquat campaigns due to keyboard-layout transliteration errors.

§2 · Affected Products

Who is at risk, and who is not.

At risk: end users on Windows who attempted to install Claude for Windows and reached the typosquat domain instead of the legitimate Anthropic landing page. Risk is highest for users who searched for terms like "claude windows download" on consumer search engines, where typosquat domains can rank highly in promoted slots.

✓

TEOS stack & Elmahrosa products are NOT affected

None of Elmahrosa's products are affected by this campaign. TEOS Sentinel Shield, TEOS AI Engine, UCH Sovereign Core, and TEOS Comply-Crawl have no Windows desktop distribution and do not share a supply chain with the malicious installer. This advisory is informational for the broader Claude ecosystem.

The legitimate Claude desktop application is published by Anthropic, PBC and is only available from claude.com/download. Anthropic-signed installers carry an Authenticode signature from Anthropic; the malicious installer does not.

§3 · Technical Details

How the impersonation works.

The typosquat operates through a combination of brand impersonation, search-engine seeding, and unsigned-binary delivery:

  • Domain anatomy — clude-promo-offer.com drops the a in claude and appends a marketing-style suffix (promo-offer) to suggest a legitimate giveaway or discount campaign.
  • Visual impersonation — the landing page reuses Anthropic colors, fonts, and product imagery without authorization. It includes a fake download CTA that points to an unsigned Windows executable.
  • Unsigned binary — the delivered .exe is not signed by Anthropic. Windows SmartScreen will warn on first execution, but motivated users may dismiss the warning if they believe the source is legitimate.
  • Post-install behavior (preliminary, under investigation) — credential harvesting from browser stores, scheduled-task persistence, outbound traffic to a C2 endpoint over HTTPS, and the absence of any actual Claude functionality.

§5 · Recommended Actions

What to do now.

  • Block the domain — add clude-promo-offer.com (and any wildcard at *.clude-promo-offer.com) to your DNS sinkhole, web proxy, and EDR blocklists.
  • Verify your Claude installer source — only install Claude for Windows from claude.com/download. Verify the Authenticode signature is issued to Anthropic, PBC before running.
  • If a host downloaded the binary — isolate the host, run a full EDR scan, rotate any credentials entered while logged into the host, revoke active session tokens (browser, SSO), and review browser autofill stores.
  • Educate users — particularly Arabic-speaking users and users on mobile, where typosquats are harder to detect visually. Show them the correct domain (claude.com) and the missing letter in the typosquat.
  • Report further sightings — if you encounter related typosquats or other distribution channels for the same payload, report them to Anthropic security and to Elmahrosa at the contacts below.
