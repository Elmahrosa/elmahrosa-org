### AI Identification
- **Requirement**: Interactive AI systems must clearly identify themselves as AI.  
- **TEOS Enforcement**:  
  - Sentinel prompts enforce disclosure at system level.  
  - All interactive agents prepend identity markers in responses.  

---

### Content Marking
- **Requirement**: AI‑generated and AI‑altered content must be marked with machine‑readable tags.  
- **TEOS Enforcement**:  
  - Repository mesh auto‑marks outputs with metadata.  
  - CI/CD hooks prevent merges without content markers.  

---

### Deepfake Labelling
- **Requirement**: Synthetic or manipulated media must be explicitly labelled.  
- **TEOS Enforcement**:  
  - Governance modules insert “Deepfake” labels.  
  - Audit logs capture provenance and disclosure.  

---

### Biometric & Emotion Disclosure
- **Requirement**: Emotion recognition and biometric categorisation must be disclosed.  
- **TEOS Enforcement**:  
  - Compliance tooling logs biometric/emotion usage.  
  - Disclosure banners enforced at runtime.
