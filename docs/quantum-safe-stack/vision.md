# Elmahrosa Quantum-Safe Vision

Elmahrosa International is building sovereign-grade AI and security infrastructure for the MENA region. As quantum computing advances toward breaking classical encryption (RSA/ECC), organizations across the region — government, finance, healthcare — face a "harvest now, decrypt later" exposure window.

Our quantum-safe roadmap exists to ensure Elmahrosa's own products (TEOS Sentinel Shield, UnityCare, DealMaker) and any institutional clients we serve have a credible, crypto-agile migration path — built on open NIST standards, not proprietary lock-in.

This is architecture and roadmap work, not a claim of deployed quantum hardware. Our approach: document today, design for crypto-agility, migrate incrementally as standards and infrastructure mature — sovereignty and independence first.
