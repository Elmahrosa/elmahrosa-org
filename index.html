The Company

From Alexandria, to the world.

Elmahrosa International was founded in 2007 in Alexandria as an events and production company. In 2020, the company pivoted to software and AI development — launching its first technical products and establishing the engineering foundation that became the TEOS stack.

The thesis is simple: sovereign AI infrastructure is a global category, and the next generation of it would not be built only in San Francisco or London — it would also be built where the most demanding institutional buyers actually live.

We build for governments, hospitals, ministries, and banks worldwide. The TEOS stack is the result: a coherent set of production systems, each addressing one well-defined institutional need, each engineered to run inside the customer's perimeter — and stronger when run together.
